## Interview Process
Our interview process for applicants spans two days and focuses on assessing coding skills through a hands-on coding interview. To streamline this process, we’ve created an [Interview Task Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Ak_jTdTAa07mL8VvvwFGzNabdUtYFPPfATa6fYEV9KQ/edit?usp=sharing) that outlines everything you need to know, including:
- Step-by-step guidance on conducting the interview.

- Pre- and post-interview tasks, such as sending sample emails to applicants before each interview day.

- Instructions for merging the applicant’s pull request (PR) and revoking their access to the interview repository once the process concludes.

From the applicant’s perspective, the interview is designed as a series of interconnected tasks that build progressively. This approach simulates a cohesive, real-world development scenario, offering a practical alternative to standalone technical questions. It allows us to evaluate not just their coding ability, but also their problem-solving process and adaptability in a structured, meaningful context.
